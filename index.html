Privacy Policy – ButceTakip

Last updated: March 25, 2026

ButceTakip is a personal finance application designed to help users track income, expenses, credit cards, budgets and assets.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when using the ButceTakip application.

1. Data Storage

All financial data entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device. This includes:

  • Income and expense records
  • Accounts and credit card data
  • Categories
  • Asset records
  • Backup files created by the user

ButceTakip does not automatically upload or store user financial data on external servers.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

ButceTakip does not sell, rent, or share user financial data with third parties.

No personal financial information is transmitted to the developer’s servers.

3. Purchases and Billing

If the user purchases a Pro subscription or premium feature, transactions are processed by Google Play Billing.

This may include purchase status, subscription information and transaction identifiers.

ButceTakip does not store or access users' payment card details.

4. Backups and Exported Data

The application allows users to export data, create backups, and share files.

These files are fully controlled by the user and are not automatically transmitted.

5. Accounts

The application does not require user account registration to function.

6. Notifications

If enabled by the user, the app may send local notifications for reminders and financial tracking purposes.

These notifications are generated on the device and are not sent from external servers.

7. Third-Party Services

ButceTakip may use platform services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing

These services may process limited technical or transaction-related data according to their own policies.

8. Data Deletion

Users can delete their data by:

  • Removing records inside the app
  • Deleting exported files
  • Uninstalling the application

9. Security

Users are responsible for securing their own devices and backup files.

10. Children's Privacy

ButceTakip is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected on this page.
