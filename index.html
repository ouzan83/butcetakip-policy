Privacy Policy – ButceTakip

Last updated: March 15, 2026

ButceTakip is a personal finance management application designed to help users track income, expenses, assets and financial activities.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the ButceTakip application.

1. Data Storage

All financial data entered into the application, including income, expenses, accounts, assets and categories, is stored locally on the user's device.

ButceTakip does not automatically upload or store user financial data on external servers.

2. User Control of Data

Users have full control over their data. The application may allow users to:

  • Export financial data
  • Create backup files
  • Share exported files

Any exported files are controlled and managed by the user.

3. Accounts

The current version of the application does not require mandatory user accounts.

Future features such as Google Sign-In or cloud synchronization may be introduced.

4. Data Collection

ButceTakip does not sell, trade, or distribute user financial data.

Future versions may collect anonymous technical data to improve stability and performance.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • App Store services
  • External payment platforms

6. Security

Users are responsible for the security of their devices and exported backup files.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions.
